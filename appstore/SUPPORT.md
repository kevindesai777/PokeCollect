# PokeCollect support

PokeCollect is a free, unofficial fan app for tracking your card collection, with an AI that runs
on your phone. I'm Kevin Desai, and I made it.

## Get in touch

- Email: kevindesai777@gmail.com
- Bugs and ideas: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/kevindesai777/PokeCollect/issues)

## Common questions

**Questions in my own words don't work on my iPhone.** They need Apple Intelligence: an iPhone 15
Pro or newer, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Right after you turn it on, iOS still
has to download its model, so try again a few minutes later. Your collection, search and adding
cards by name work on every iPhone.

**The mic doesn't hear me.** Allow the microphone and speech recognition for PokeCollect in
Settings. Recognition runs on the iPhone, in English.

**Where is my collection stored?** Only on your phone. There's no account, and nothing is sent
anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your collection.
